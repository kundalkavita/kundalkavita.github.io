Current work

Department of Radiology, University of California, Davis

Brain–peripheral inflammatory interactions in autoimmune arthritides

Autoimmune arthritis is driven by inflammation in the joints, but its effects reach the brain too. I study how peripheral inflammation and the brain influence each other, using medical imaging to trace these interactions and understand their role in disease.

PhD research

IIT Hyderabad · Computational Genomics and Transcriptomics Laboratory · Supervisor: Dr. Rahul Kumar

Radiogenomics modeling to identify biomarkers of survival outcome in glioblastoma patients

Glioblastoma is one of the most lethal cancers worldwide, with poor prognosis. Genetic alterations in glioblastoma patients show distinct phenotypes that can be seen non-invasively through medical imaging. Radiogenomics explores this link between genomic and imaging features.

My work relates radiomic features from patients' MRI to genomic data (mutations and copy-number variation) to find prognostic biomarkers. The aim is radiogenomic characterization of glioblastoma tumors and earlier diagnosis, for better disease management and palliative care.
