I study how the genetics of a brain tumor shows up in its MRI scans — using radiogenomics and machine learning to find markers that predict survival in glioblastoma without a biopsy.

I'm a PhD scholar under the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF) in the Computational Genomics and Transcriptomics Laboratory at IIT Hyderabad, supervised by Dr. Rahul Kumar. Before that I earned an M.Tech in Biotechnology and Medical Engineering at NIT Rourkela (2021) and a B.Tech in Biotechnology at SBSSU Gurdaspur, Punjab (2019).

My path runs from the wet lab to the computer: recombinant protein production and purification, antimicrobial and susceptibility testing, molecular dynamics simulation for peptide design, and now deep learning on multimodal medical images and multi-omics data.

Research

PhD thesis · IIT Hyderabad · 2022–present

Radiogenomics modeling to identify biomarkers of survival outcome in glioblastoma patients

Glioblastoma is among the most lethal cancers. Its genetic alterations leave visible traces in medical images, so I link radiomic features from patient MRI with genomic data (mutations and copy-number variation) to find prognostic biomarkers — aiming for earlier, non-invasive diagnosis and better disease management.

Imaging & AI
