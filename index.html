Kavita Kundal

Department of Radiology, University of California, Davis

I study how the brain and the body's immune system talk to each other in autoimmune arthritis — using imaging to understand how inflammation in the joints and the brain shape one another.

I work in the Department of Radiology at UC Davis on brain–peripheral inflammatory interactions in autoimmune arthritides. Before UC Davis, I was a PhD scholar under the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF) in the Computational Genomics and Transcriptomics Laboratory at IIT Hyderabad, supervised by Dr. Rahul Kumar, where I worked on radiogenomics of glioblastoma. Earlier I earned an M.Tech in Biotechnology and Medical Engineering at NIT Rourkela (2021) and a B.Tech in Biotechnology at SBSSU Gurdaspur, Punjab (2019).

My path runs from the wet lab to the computer: recombinant protein production and purification, antimicrobial susceptibility testing and serotyping, molecular dynamics simulation for peptide design, deep learning on multimodal MRI and multi-omics data, and now imaging of neuroinflammation.
